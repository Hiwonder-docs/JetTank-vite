14.1 Basic Voice Lesson ​

14.1.1 6-Microphone Array Introduction ​

14.1.1.1 Introduction ​

Literally, this 6-microphone array is composed of 6 microphone, and system that samples and processes spatial characteristics of sound field. It can locate sound source, suppress background noise, interference, reverberation and echo, extract and separate signal. Its circular plane design contributes to 360 degree recording.

14.1.1.2 Specification ​

The module structure is as pictured.

InterfaceFunction
USB interfaceConnect to PC or embedded devices
ADFUFor flashing. Press this key when inserting the USB to enter developer mode
Reference signal interfacecancel echo
Microphone serial numberNumbers 0-5 are printed on module
LED serial numberled is numbered from 0 to 11 clockwise

14.1.1.3 Wiring ​

NOTE

there are plenty of metal parts on the bottom of the microphone array. You need to place it on dry and clean desktop when using. Don’t let metal objects touch it, otherwise it will get short circuit and damaged.

Connect J1 on microphone array and USB interface of external device through USB type-A cable. The voltage and current required to be 5V and 0.5A. It can be connected to Pi4 or Jetson Nano for audio data and control signal transmission.

Connect J2 and the back end of external device i.e. SPK+ and SPK- of speaker, with 2PIN wire to realize echo canceling and reference signal transmission. The peak value of reference signal cannot exceed 300mV.

Take PC computer for example.

NOTE

①If you speak no words after waking up the microphone, it will enter sleep mode, so you need to wake up the microphone again according to instructions in “1. voice wake up”.

②Please speak loudly and in appropriate speed, otherwise microphone cannot recognize your words or just recognize one part of your words.

③Please convey your voice command one by one that is you should continue to say next voice command after the recognition result of last voice command is printed

For example, you can say “come back”, “yellow” and “turn off the light”. After microphone recognizes your voice commands, following feedback will given.

  • Identify success: whether your voice commands are identified by microphone.

  • Confidence of keywords: credibility of keywords recognition result.

  • Keywords recognition result: if the keywords confidence is too low, your voice command will not be displayed on the terminal.

There are two types of English voice commands, namely combination or non-combination voice command.

Combination voice command: A (start) + B(end), for example turn on(A) air conditioner(B).

Non-combination: yellow, turn off the light, etc. You need to say it all at once. If you just say “Turn off”, microphone cannot recognize your command.

14.1.6 Sound Source Localization ​

14.1.6.1 Sound Source Localization Logic ​

Sound localization refers to the ability to identify the location of a sound source in a sound field. There are two kinds of sound localization, including microphone array and sound intensity probe.

  1. Microphone array consists of several or thousands of microphones that arranged in sequence. These microphones collect sound at the same time. As each microphone is placed in different position, the phases of audio they obtained are also different. Then it calculates position of sound source based on the phase difference.

2）Sound intensity probe is composed of two microphones that face to each other. It can measure sound pressure, size and direction of sound wave vibration at each point in the space. Besides, scan and test near the surface or envelope of the measured object to get how the sound field distributes near the measured object, so as to grasp the location and distribution of the noise source.

14.1.6.2 Sound Localization Outcome ​

JetAuto employs microphone array to localize the sound source. You can refer to “Lesson 5 Wake up Microphone” to wake up the microphone, and it will perform sound source localization. Take awaking NO.5 microphone for example. Following messages will pop up when microphone array is awaken.

The fifth microphone is awaken: as the sound source is closest to NO.5 microphone, this microphone is set main microphone for better reception.

Wake-up angle: 306. Calculate the sound source location and angle of microphone array center based on the phase difference between sound collected by multiple microphones.

N0.10 LED is on. There are 12 LEDs on this 6-microphone array. LED will light up according to the wake-up angle.
