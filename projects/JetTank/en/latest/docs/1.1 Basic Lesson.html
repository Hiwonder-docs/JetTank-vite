1.1 Basic Lesson ​

1.1.1 JetTank Introduction ​

1.1.1.1 Product Introduction ​

JetTank is a ROS tank robot tailored for ROS learning. It is loaded with NVIDIA Jetson Nano, high-performance encoder motor, Lidar, 3D depth camera and 7-inch LCD screen, which open up more functionalities. Robot motion control, mapping and navigation, path planning, tracking and obstacle avoidance, autonomous driving, human feature recognition, somatosensory interaction and voice interaction all can be achieved.

1.1.1.2 Packing List ​

1.1.1.3 Tutorial Overview ​

STEP 1: JetTank Introduction

JetTank will disconnect and power off before shipping. Therefore, please firstly learn how to charge robot and install accessories according to the lessons in folder “1. Getting Started”

When assembly is completed, learn about the robot status after first boot up and quickly start to quickly experiencing robot via APP.

STEP 2: Basic Lesson Learning or Other Functions Leaning

After experience robot via APP, if you want to understand the following robot lesson better, you can go to “2. Linux Basic Lesson” and “6.Jetson Nano and Jetson Nano Expansion board” to build the foundation. Please note that the image used in these lesson are ubuntu official system image which is used with virtual machine, not the robot image, so do not confuse them.

STEP 3: Please go to the folder “7. Motion Control Lesson” to learn “1. Basic Control Lesson” and “Simulation Model and URDF” in sequence.

In “1. Basic Control Lesson”, you will learn encoder motor driver, chassis car, PID, IMU and other basic knowledge to get further understanding of motion control.

In “Simulation Model and URDF”, learn about the model files that describe all the elements of the robot in ROS, learn the use of the simulation software gazebo, and acquire knowledge of simulating robot motion.

STEP 4: Users can go to “8. Depth Camera” to learn the basics of depth camera and prepare for the subsequent AI Vision Games combined with depth camera.

Please note that JetTank is shipped with the depth camera already configured, so the first three lessons are basic general lesson, which aim to help users understand the structure and configuration of depth camera. It also needs to be accompanied by the official ubuntu system image used in "2. Linux Basic Lesson - ROS Basic Lesson".

You can learn the first three lessons according to your needs after learning "Lesson 4 Camera Calibration" and "Lesson 5 Data Types and Point Cloud".

STEP 5: OpenCV Lesson

Learn the basic of OpenCV in folder “9. OpenCV Vision Recognition Lesson”. With project getting further, some practical operation will be performed to verify the research theory of machine vision.

STEP 6: Machine Learning Lesson

In folder “10. ROS+Machine Learning Lesson”, you will understand machine learning and libraries used, and master it through practical operation.

STEP 7: AI Creative Lesson

In folder “11. AI Creative Lesson”, you will learn how to use command line to start AI vision games and have a simple understanding of the implementation logic of each game, as well as nurture a mindset for modifying program.

STEP 8: Lidar Lesson

In folder “12. Lidar Lesson”, you will learn Lidar basic, navigation and mapping algorithms to understand how robot to navigate and map in a unknown surroundings.

STEP 9: Robots Formation Lesson

In folder “13. Robots Formation Lesson”, you will learn how to configure multi-robot and group control.

STEP 10:Voice Control Lesson

In folder “14. Voice Control Lesson”, you will have a rudimentary knowledge of voice module and apply it into project operation.

1.1.2 Device Charging ​

1.1.2.1 Charging Notice ​

JetTank adopts 11.1V 6000mAh Lipo battery for power supply, and please charge the battery with the provided charger .

For delivery safety, the robot must be powered off, and the battery cannot be fully charged. Therefore, you need to charge the battery first when you receive the robot, and it takes about 2 hours to fully charge the battery.

When the battery voltage is less than overdischarge voltage, robot will turn off automatically to protect battery. At this time, please fully charge the battery

To ensure the normal performance of the robot, please charge the battery in time when the voltage is below 10V.

1.1.2.2 Operation Steps ​

  1. Push the switch on JetTank to “OFF” side.
  1. Then check the bottom of the robot to connect the battery wire. Connect the red wire to the red wire on Jetson Nano expansion board, and black to black.
  1. Next, insert the charger into the DC connector, as the figure shown below:
  1. When the indicator on the charger is red, the battery is being charged. When it turns green, the battery is fully charged.

NOTE

please remove the charger in time when the battery is fully charged to avoid overcharge.

1.1.2.3 Notices for Lipo Battery ​

  1. Please do not directly connect the power cable to the battery. Otherwise the power cable will short circuit due to the connection between the positive polarity and negative polarity.

  2. Please use the charger comes with JetAuto to charge.

  3. When the charger only connects to the battery and is not plugged into an AC power outlet, its indicator will turn green.

  4. If the robot is not used for a long time, please fully charge the robot and then disconnect the battery wire connecting the expansion board and the battery.

  5. Keep the battery far away from high temperature and all kinds of liquids to prevent overheating, fire or damp, which results in function decline and short service life.

  6. Do not hit, throw or step on the battery.

  7. Do not use the battery in the environment of strong static electricity or strong magnetic field, otherwise the safety protection device of the battery will be easily damaged.

  8. Do not use metal objects to connect the positive and negative poles of the battery.

  9. Do not insert the battery into the socket.

1.1.3 First Start JetTank ​

1.1.3.1 Notes of Usage ​

  1. For the normal performance of the robot, please charge the battery in time when it is below 10V.

  2. Please do not place the robot on the edge of the table to avoid the robot from falling and being damaged.

  3. The servo will heat up after the robot works for a long time. And you need to power off the robot to let the servo rest. When the servos cool down, the robot can work again.

1.1.3.2 Start JetAuto ​

  1. Place JetTank on a flat surface, and turn on the switch on Jetson Nano expansion board. Then the red LED light on the switch will keep on.
  1. Please wait for a while till blue ELD light (LED1) on the expansion board lights up, and keeps flashing, and the buzzer sounds. At this time, the robot starts successfully.
  1. Then it will generate a WiFi starting with HW. Connect to this WiFi, and the default password is “hiwonder”

For how to use APP to control robot, you can refer to the material in “Lesson 5- Lesson 7” under the same folder.

For how to connect to the system desktop through the remote tool, you can check the instruction in “7. Motion Control Lesson->1. Basic Control Lesson-Lesson 1 Build Development Environment”.

1.1.4 APP Installation ​

  2. Before using WonderAi, enable GPS and Wi-Fi.

  3. Android user: the installation package can be found in “1. Getting Started/ Lesson 5 APP Installation/ APP Installation Package” and transfer it to your phone to install.

  4. iOS user: download and install “WonderAi” in App store.

1.1.5.2 Network Mode Introduction ​

Two network modes of robot:

AP direct connection mode: development board is able to generate a WiFi which can connect with your phone. (but no internet connection)

  1. STA LAN mode: development board can connect to the specific hotspot and WiFi (Internet can be connected)

  2. JetTank defaults to AP direct connection mode. Two modes are the same to the realization of robot functions.

It is recommended to learn the configuration of direct connection mode first to experience AI games, then LAN mode connection can be your reference.

1.1.5.3 Direct Connection Mode (Must-read) ​

Take iOS system for example ( same for Android system).

  1. Open “WonderAi” APP, then tap “Advanced Platform” and “JetTank” in sequence.

NOTE

if you choose “LAN mode”, please refer to the file in “6. Jetson Nano and Jetson Nano Expansion Board->1. Jetson Nano Basic Lesson->Lesson 7 Jetson Nano Network Connection” to connect the network.

  1. Next, click “Go to connect device hotspots” to join the WiFi generated by JetTank.
  1. The device WiFi starts with “HW” and the password is “hiwonder”.

NOTE

for iOS user, please do not return back to the APP until the WiFi icon**appears above, otherwise JetTank cannot be searched. If JetTank cannot be searched, please click**to refresh.

  1. Return to the APP, and click the robotic icon to enter game selection interface.

NOTE

If the window showing “No Internet Connection. Whether to keep connection” pops up, just select “Keep Connection”.

  1. When the following window pops up, it means that you have selected a wrong version. Click “Confirm”, then APP will automatically switch to the home interface of the right version.
  1. The home interface of the advanced kit is shown below:
  1. The specific operation method of each game can be viewed in “1. Getting Started/ Lesson 1 APP Remote Control/ Lesson 7 APP Control”.

1.1.5.4 LAN Mode (Optional) ​

  1. Connect your phone to 5G network first. Here take “Hiwonder_5G” for example. (Dual-band router is supported. In this case, the name of Wi-Fi will be distinguished by default. For example, Hiwonder is 2.4GHz and Hiwonder_5G is 5GHz. )
  1. Open “WonderAi” APP, then tap “Advanced Platform” and “JetTank” in sequence.
  1. Click “+” button, and select “LAN Mode”.
  1. Enter the password of WiFi connected (wrong password will fail to connect). Then click “Next”.
  1. Next, click “Go to connect device hotspots”.
  1. Your phone will jump to WIFI connection interface. Connect to the WIFI starting with “HW” and enter the password “hiwonder”. Then return to app.
  1. You can find that APP is connecting with robot.
  1. After a while, a robotic icon will appear and the LED on expansion board is lighting up.
  1. Long press robotic icon to check the IP address and ID of device.

  1. Search IP address on remote connection tool, then you can connect robot via VNC remote tool. The specific method can refer to the content in file “7. Motion Control Lesson/ Lesson 1 Set Development Environment”.

  2. If you want to switch to direct connection mode, long press KEY1 on expansion board until the blue light flashes.

  • Operation Steps and Outcome

Lidar has three modes including “Avoid obstacle”, “Lidar follow” and “Lidar guarding”.

Avoid obstacle

  1. Click “Avoid obstacle” button to start this game. Then the robot will keep going forward.

  2. When detecting the obstacle, the robot will turn automatically to avoid the obstacle.

Lidar guarding

After the game starts, JetTank will adjust its body to face the obstacle when detecting the obstacle.

1.1.7 Wireless Handle Control ​

1.1.7.1 Getting Ready ​

NOTE

Please insert the handle receiver first before turning on robot (Robot has been inserted with handle receiver before shipping). In addition, when loading batteries, do not invert the position and negative poles.

  1. Insert the handle receiver into one of USB ports on JetTank (This step had been prepared before you got robot).

  2. Prepare your own two AAA batteries. Remove the back cover of the handle and load them into the battery slot.

1.1.7.2 Device Connection ​

  1. Boot up JetTank. After the robot starts successfully, turn on the handle. At this time, two LED lights will flash simultaneously.

  2. Please wait for a while. Then the robot will pair with the handle automatically. After successful pairing, the green light will keep lighting up and red LED light goes off.

NOTE

  1. If the handle doesn’t connect with the robot within 30s or there is no operation on the handle within 5 minutes after turning on, it will enter sleep mode. And you can press “START” to activate the handle.

  2. If the robot has no reaction when you press the button, you can enable the related service. For the detailed instruction, you can refer to the file in “7. Motion Control Lesson->1. Basic Control Lesson->Lesson 9 Handle Control”.
